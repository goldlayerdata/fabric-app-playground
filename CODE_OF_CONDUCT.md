# Code of conduct

This project follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests or discussions here agrees to it.

In short:

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning, and so are the maintainers.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with a step by citing a source, not by judging whoever wrote it.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks, and no unwelcome sexual attention.
- Don't publish anyone's private information, such as names, emails, workplaces or ids, without their permission.

## Reporting

**If something here makes you uncomfortable**, contact the maintainers privately through the Gold Layer accounts linked on [goldlayerdata.com](https://goldlayerdata.com/).

**For a problem with content rather than conduct**, open an issue.

Reports are read by the maintainers and handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep breaking it, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
